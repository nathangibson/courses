## 📈 Review: Short-form Cultural Intelligence Scale (SFCQ): Background

- Intended to distinguish capability from motivation
- "intelligence": "the abilities necessary for adaptation to an environmental context" (Thomas et al. 2015 citing Binet & Simon, 1916; Sternberg, 1997; Wechsler, 1939).
- 3526 participants in 14 samples

## 📈 Review: Intercultural Sensitivity Scale: Background

Authors wanted to reconcile affective measurements with behavioral measurements. (Fritz et al. 2002)

## 📈 Review: Intercultural Sensitivity Scale: Background

- **Intercultural competence** in 3 dimensions
	- **awareness** (self and culture)
	- **sensitivity**/affective (self-esteem, self-monitoring, empathy, open-mindedness, nonjudgmental, and social relaxation)
	- **adroitness**/behavioral (message skills, appropriate self-disclosure, behavioral flexibility, and interaction management)

(Fritz et al. 2002)
