- Arguments

## Course Visit & Interview Prep: Questions 1

- How do you develop a plan together with community stakeholders when there is a mismatch between your values (or those of your donors) and theirs?
- How do you resolve conflicts in a culturally sensitive way when it comes to issues of direct/indirect communication and face-saving? 
- To what extent do you work within existing norms and hierarchies (gender roles, religious authorities) even if you consider them unjust? 

## Course Visit & Interview Prep: Questions 2

- In what ways have specific religious institutions, communities, practices, etc. been a supportive resource in your work? And where have you felt them as a hindrance? 
- If someone is personally religious, how would you suggest they approach a job such as yours, especially in communication with partners? Should they be open about their perspectives? What if they are not religious?
