## Intercultural Sensitivity Scale: Background

Authors wanted to reconcile affective measurements with behavioral measurements. (Fritz et al. 2002)

## Intercultural Sensitivity Scale: Background

- **Intercultural competence** in 3 dimensions
	- **awareness** (self and culture)
	- **sensitivity**/affective (self-esteem, self-monitoring, empathy, open-mindedness, nonjudgmental, and social relaxation)
	- **adroitness**/behavioral (message skills, appropriate self-disclosure, behavioral flexibility, and interaction management)

(Fritz et al. 2002)

## Autobiography of Intercultural Encounters: Discussion

- Was it hard to choose an experience? Why did you choose the one you did?
- **Feelings (affective)**: What were your feelings during and after the encounter? What did you understand of the other person's feelings? What are your feelings now reflecting on it?
- **Behavior**: How did you adapt your behavior? What would you do differently? What surprised you about the other person's behavior? 

## Autobiography of Intercultural Encounters: Discussion

- **Knowledge & skills (cognitive)**: What did you already know about the other person's culture? What did you learn about it through the experience? What do you know now that would have helped? What do you think they knew about your culture?
- **Action**: What 1 thing would you do to prepare for the next such encounter?
